Save Time. Do Good.

The screen time app where extra minutes cost money, and all profits go to charity.

We believe the best way to keep you off your phone is having to pay for extra minutes.

So why not use that money to do good? We give all profits to charity.

Either you spend less time on your phone, or you’re doing good.

Save Time. Do Good.

How it works

  1. Set your limits

    Pick the apps that eat your time and give each one a daily limit.

  2. Hit the limit

    The app locks. Wait until midnight, or buy 5 or 20 extra minutes if you really need them.

  3. Pick a charity

    Choose where our profits go. You can change it anytime in Settings.

We keep none
of the profit.

We give all profits to charity. Profits are what is left after Apple’s commission and taxes. We donate the profits at the end of each year, and publish the receipts here afterwards.
